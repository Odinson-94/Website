Pricing questions

Before you choose a plan.

Prices are shown in GBP excluding VAT. Stripe calculates and collects applicable tax only where the Adelphos Stripe account has been configured to do so.

What is a Usage Credit?

A Usage Credit is a unit in your Adelphos balance, used for app reports and measured AI usage. Published report prices show credits per calculation or project. GBP equivalents use the current top-up pack price; subscription allowances can have a different effective price.

What happens when I reach my allowance?

Additional paid usage requires an explicit top-up or upgrade through Stripe. Adelphos does not create an unapproved overage charge.

How is my subscription activated?

Stripe sends a signed webhook after successful Checkout. Adelphos verifies the purchased Stripe Price and binds the entitlement to the signed-in user and tenant. The return page alone cannot activate access.

Can I cancel or update payment details?

Yes. Sign in to Adelphos Chat and open Account Settings, then Billing. Adelphos creates a customer-specific Stripe portal session after checking account ownership. Cancellation normally takes effect at the end of the paid billing period. See the Cancellation and Refund Policy.

Do free calculators recommend products?

No. Public calculator pages perform focused calculations only. They do not reproduce restricted selectors or recommend manufacturer products.

Is engineering output automatically suitable for issue?

No. The user remains responsible for checking inputs, assumptions, standards, results and suitability, and for obtaining competent-person review where required.
