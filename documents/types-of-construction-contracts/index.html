Procurement and contract forms

What are the main types of construction contracts?

Construction contract types can be described by procurement route, pricing mechanism and standard form. Those choices overlap, so the project team should test them together rather than select a contract by name alone.

Traditional construction contracts

The employer normally retains the design team and appoints a contractor to build the developed design. The contract must define any contractor-designed portions, measurement basis and administration duties.

Design and build contracts

The contractor accepts defined design and construction responsibility against the employer's requirements and contractor's proposals. Scope clarity and the treatment of existing design are critical.

Management contracting and construction management

Management routes package the work and support earlier starts, but the contractual relationships, cost risk and employer involvement differ. The team needs suitable management capacity and clear package coordination.

Lump-sum, measurement and cost-reimbursable contracts

Pricing can be fixed against a defined scope, measured from completed quantities, target-cost or reimbursed from allowable cost. Each approach allocates uncertainty and cost control differently.

JCT construction contracts

The JCT family includes forms for several procurement and project arrangements. The selected form, particulars, schedules and amendments must reflect the actual project and current edition.

NEC construction contracts

The NEC family uses defined core clauses, main and secondary options and active administration processes. The chosen contract, pricing option and contract data must operate together.

Frequently asked questions.

What are the main types of construction contracts?

Common approaches include traditional, design and build, management contracting, construction management, target-cost, cost-reimbursable, lump-sum and measurement contracts. Standard forms such as JCT and NEC can support different procurement and pricing arrangements.

What is a traditional construction contract?

Under a traditional route, the employer usually appoints the design team and the contractor constructs the completed design. The detailed form determines design portions, risk and administration.

What is a design and build contract?

A design and build contract places defined design and construction responsibility with the contractor, subject to the employer's requirements, contractor's proposals and the negotiated terms.

What is the difference between JCT and NEC contracts?

JCT and NEC are families of standard forms with different structures, terminology and administration. The correct choice and option depend on the procurement strategy, project risk and how the parties intend to manage time, cost and change.
