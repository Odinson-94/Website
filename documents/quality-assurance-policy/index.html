What it does

Use Quality Assurance Policy to turn a project brief, verified source records and issue requirements into a structured draft for responsible review.

Inputs you provide

Provide the brief, verified project facts, applicable references, template requirements, audience and issue or approval status.

Output you receive

Adelphos returns a structured draft and supporting record that can be checked, revised and issued through the organisation's own controls.

Limits and responsibility

The output is a working draft, not automatic professional, regulatory, legal or organisational approval. A responsible reviewer controls issue and use.
