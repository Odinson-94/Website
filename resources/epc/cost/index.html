Direct answer

How much does an Energy Performance Certificate cost?

There is no single government-set EPC assessment price. GOV.UK states that the cost varies by assessor and property size, while commercial EPC cost depends on the building being assessed. Get a scope and price from an appropriately accredited assessor for the correct property type.

Property type and size

A home and a complex business property do not have the same survey scope or assessor requirements.

Assessor and location

Assessment fees are agreed with the assessor. Check accreditation and what the quoted service includes.

Existing certificate

Search the official register first. The property may already have a certificate suitable for the immediate task.

Frequently asked questions about energy performance certificate cost.

Is there a fixed EPC price?

No. GOV.UK says assessment cost varies by assessor and property size. Commercial guidance says cost depends on the building being assessed.

Should I check for an existing EPC before paying?

Yes. Search the responsible official register first and confirm whether the existing certificate is valid and suitable for your task.

Does Adelphos sell EPC assessments?

This page does not advertise an assessor appointment or fixed assessment fee. Adelphos provides calculation workflows and scenario review; official certification follows the accredited-assessor route.

Does Adelphos issue an official EPC?

No. An official EPC must be produced and lodged through the applicable accredited-assessor process. Adelphos can calculate and compare supported scenarios, but its calculation output is not a lodged certificate.
