Direct answer

What is the correct way to get an EPC?

First check the official register for an existing certificate. If the property needs a new EPC, arrange an assessment with an accredited assessor for the correct building type and jurisdiction. The assessor completes the assessment and provides the certificate through the applicable official process.

Check before commissioning

An existing certificate may already be available from the official register.

Use an accredited assessor

The official service lets users find an assessor for a home, business property or public building.

Provide real building evidence

Access and supporting information help the assessor record the property accurately.

Frequently asked questions about how to get an EPC.

Who can produce an EPC?

An EPC must be produced through the applicable accredited-assessor process. Use the official assessor search for the jurisdiction and building type.

Can I download an existing EPC?

Yes. GOV.UK says an existing certificate can be checked and downloaded free through its certificate service for England, Wales and Northern Ireland. Scotland uses its own register.

What if the property has no EPC?

Use the official route to find an accredited assessor and arrange a new assessment. Do not substitute a modelled estimate for a lodged certificate.

Does Adelphos issue an official EPC?

No. An official EPC must be produced and lodged through the applicable accredited-assessor process. Adelphos can calculate and compare supported scenarios, but its calculation output is not a lodged certificate.
