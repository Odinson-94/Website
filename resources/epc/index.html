Direct answer

What does an energy performance certificate mean?

An Energy Performance Certificate records an assessed building energy rating and related information under the method and jurisdiction shown on that certificate. For domestic properties in England and Wales, the familiar energy-efficiency bands run from A, the most efficient, to G, the least efficient. Scotland has its own register and guidance.

Frequently asked questions about energy performance certificate meaning.

What is an EPC?

An EPC is an Energy Performance Certificate. It records an assessed energy rating for a building and includes information that must be read in the context of its method, date and jurisdiction.

Are EPC ratings the same throughout the UK?

Do not assume one register or one current process covers every UK jurisdiction. England, Wales and Northern Ireland use the GOV.UK certificate service, while Scotland has a separate EPC register and guidance.

Does Adelphos issue an official EPC?

No. An official EPC must be produced and lodged through the applicable accredited-assessor process. Adelphos can calculate and compare supported scenarios, but its calculation output is not a lodged certificate.
