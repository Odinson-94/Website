Direct answer

What does an EPC F rating mean?

For the domestic Energy Efficiency Rating used in England and Wales, EPC band F covers scores 21 to 38 and is the second-lowest domestic energy-efficiency band. The letter alone does not identify the right improvement. Read the exact score, assessment date, property data, potential rating and recommendations on the lodged certificate.

Frequently asked questions about EPC F rating.

What should I check on an EPC F rating?

An EPC F rating means the assessed domestic score falls within 21 to 38 for the England and Wales banding cited here. The next action depends on the exact certificate and building evidence.

What score is EPC band F?

The government housing glossary gives band F as 21 to 38 on the domestic Energy Efficiency Rating scale for England and Wales.

Can Adelphos model an EPC F improvement?

Adelphos can compare supported changes from a complete, version-locked assessment owned by the authenticated user. The output is a scenario calculation, not a new official EPC.

Can an EPC band change be guaranteed?

No. A band depends on the assessed building data and the applicable calculation method. Model a defined change, check the evidence, and use an accredited assessor when an official certificate is required.
