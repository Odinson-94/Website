Direct answer

How do you find an Energy Performance Certificate?

For a property in England, Wales or Northern Ireland, use the GOV.UK Find an energy certificate service. It accepts a postcode, street and town, or certificate number. For Scotland, use the separate Scottish EPC Register described by Scottish Government guidance.

England, Wales and Northern Ireland

Use the GOV.UK certificate service for homes, business properties and public buildings.

Scotland

Use the Scottish EPC Register. The Scottish process and register are separate.

No valid result

If no current certificate appears, follow the official route to an accredited assessor rather than estimating a certificate.

Frequently asked questions about energy performance certificate finder.

Can I find an EPC by postcode?

Yes. The GOV.UK service supports postcode searches for England, Wales and Northern Ireland. Scottish Government guidance says the Scottish register can also be searched by postcode.

Is an EPC register search free?

The official GOV.UK service lets users view or print an existing certificate. Any fee for a new assessment is separate and is agreed with the assessor.

Why can I not find the property EPC?

The address may not have a certificate in that register, the search details may differ, or a new assessment may be needed. Check the jurisdiction and use the official help route rather than inventing a rating.

Does Adelphos issue an official EPC?

No. An official EPC must be produced and lodged through the applicable accredited-assessor process. Adelphos can calculate and compare supported scenarios, but its calculation output is not a lodged certificate.
