EPC improvement recommendations

What can improve an EPC rating?

Start with the recommendations and recorded inputs on the current certificate. Model each suitable measure before committing to work: an improvement can only be confirmed when the property is reassessed and a new EPC is lodged.

1. Check the current EPC data

Confirm floor area, construction type, insulation, glazing, heating, hot water and lighting. Incorrect source data can distort both the rating and the recommendations.

2. Improve roof and loft insulation

Review accessible roof spaces and the insulation recorded by the assessor. The appropriate build-up depends on the roof construction, moisture control and existing condition.

3. Review wall and floor insulation

Model cavity-wall, solid-wall and suspended-floor measures only where they suit the actual construction. Survey condition, ventilation and condensation risk before specifying work.

4. Reduce uncontrolled heat loss

Consider draught-proofing, doors and glazing alongside the ventilation strategy. Airtightness measures must retain safe, controlled ventilation.

5. Upgrade heating and controls

Compare the recorded heat source, distribution efficiency, time and temperature controls, zoning and emitter performance. Model the complete system rather than one product in isolation.

6. Improve domestic hot water

Review the water-heating system, cylinder insulation, primary pipe insulation and controls. The suitable measures depend on the installed system and demand.

7. Record low-energy lighting

Check the proportion of fixed low-energy outlets and make sure the assessor can verify the completed installation.

8. Model renewable generation

Solar PV and other low-carbon measures may affect the model, but orientation, shading, system size, tariffs and the calculation methodology all matter.

Use recommendations as modelling inputs, not promises

An EPC is produced under the applicable assessment methodology. Costs, savings and rating changes vary by property and assessment date; a competent assessor must verify the inputs and lodge the certificate.

What does an epc e rating mean?

An EPC E rating is the band shown on a specific lodged certificate under the applicable assessment method. Use the certificate itself for its score, assumptions and recommendations. The Adelphos scenario report compares controlled model runs but does not replace a lodged EPC.

How to get my epc from e to c?

There is no guaranteed generic route from E to C. A qualified assessor must verify the building data and proposed measures. Adelphos can calculate supported fabric scenarios from an owned assessment, but it does not promise a band change or generate an official certificate.
