Direct answers

Solar PV Calculator questions and answers.

How many solar panels do i need?

Panel count depends on usable roof area, panel rating, orientation, pitch, shading, regional yield, system losses and the design objective. The calculator produces an indicative array from explicit inputs and does not recommend a panel or inverter.

What size pv system for my roof?

Measure the genuinely usable roof area and enter the proposed panel dimensions and rating, then apply sourced orientation, regional-yield and loss assumptions. Structural capacity, access, fire safety, network and electrical design remain separate checks.
