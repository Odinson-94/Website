Calculator questions

Engineering calculator FAQs.

Are the Adelphos engineering calculators free?

The 18 public calculator pages are free to use. The Adelphos app also contains 48 live calculator entries, with some calculations available in both places.

What engineering calculations can I complete?

The public library covers HVAC, electrical, energy, lighting, solar, drainage and building-services calculations. Adelphos Chat provides additional guided sizing, selection, psychrometric, ventilation and risk-assessment workflows.

What is the difference between a free calculator and an app calculator?

A free calculator runs from a public website page. An app calculator opens the registered calculator route in Adelphos and can retain the calculation within the signed-in project workflow.

Do calculator results replace engineering review?

No. Verify the inputs, assumptions, applicable standards and output before using a result for design, procurement, installation or construction.
