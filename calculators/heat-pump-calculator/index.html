What size heat pump do i need?

Use a design heat-loss calculation at the project design condition, add any simultaneous hot-water allowance and an explicit margin, then calculate the required duty. The Adelphos tool reports that duty only and does not select equipment.

How much does a heat pump cost to run?

Estimated running cost is heat demand divided by the applicable efficiency input, multiplied by the electricity tariff, with hot-water demand treated separately where required. Use date-stamped tariffs and realistic SCOP or condition-specific performance assumptions.
